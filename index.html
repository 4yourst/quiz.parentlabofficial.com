Before you start

Is the check-in really free?

Yes, completely free, no card, no account. At the end we ask for your email so we can send you Keep Your Cool (our free guide) and a few short follow-up notes; your full results appear on screen right away either way. You can unsubscribe anytime, in one click.

What do I get at the end?

Your parent profile, a read on what's driving the friction at home, three research-backed insights, and one recommended next step, the ParentLab guide that best fits your result. One of the guides is free; the others are paid PDFs. Your results are useful either way, and there's zero obligation.

Is this a diagnosis?

No. The check-in offers general, educational guidance based on your answers, it isn't medical, psychological or diagnostic advice, and it can't assess your child. If you're worried about your child's development or wellbeing, please speak with your pediatrician.

How long does it really take?

About 2 minutes. All 9 questions are multiple-choice, and there's one optional text box at the end that you can skip.
